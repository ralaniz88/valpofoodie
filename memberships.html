Frequently asked questions

Do you charge a commission on food orders?

No. Valpo Foodie directs diners to your website or ordering provider and does not take a percentage of food orders. Your own provider fees may still apply.

Do I have to purchase a membership?

No. Basic restaurant listings remain free. Paid packages are optional marketing services. Daily soup publishing, weekly specials, happy hour promotions and dedicated online ordering buttons are premium features.

Can customers order directly from my restaurant?

Yes. Paid memberships include dedicated links to your own online ordering site, DoorDash and Uber Eats, where applicable. Basic listings retain your main website, menu, phone and directions. Valpo Foodie charges 0% order commission; fees charged by external providers still apply. Additional ordering integrations are planned.

Can Valpo Foodie update my soups and specials for me?

Yes, managed publishing is a planned optional service for paid members. Choose weekly or daily soup management, individual promotional updates, or the complete Restaurant Concierge package.

Which memberships include analytics?

Restaurant profile analytics and ordering-link click tracking are exclusive to Local Marketing Partner ($79/month) and Premier Marketing Partner ($149/month). Analytics measure interactions, not confirmed orders or sales.

Can I subscribe now?

Not yet. This page is an informational preview of proposed pricing. No checkout or payment is available.
