Frequently asked questions

Do you charge a commission on food orders?

No. Valpo Foodie directs diners to your website or ordering provider and does not take a percentage of food orders. Your own provider fees may still apply.

Do I have to purchase a membership?

No. Basic restaurant listings remain free. Paid packages are optional marketing services. Daily soup publishing, weekly specials and happy hour promotions are premium features.

Can I subscribe now?

Not yet. This page is an informational preview of proposed pricing. No checkout or payment is available.
