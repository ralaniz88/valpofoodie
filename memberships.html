Frequently asked questions

Do you charge a commission on food orders?

No. We never take a percentage of your food orders.

Do I have to purchase a membership?

No. Every restaurant can have a free basic listing. Paid plans add more ways to promote your business.

Can customers order directly from my restaurant?

Yes. Paid plans include dedicated ordering buttons for your own website and supported delivery services.

Can Valpo Foodie update my soups and specials for me?

Yes. Our planned update services include weekly or daily soup menus, specials, happy hours and full-service listing updates.

Can I see how many people view my restaurant?

Yes. The $79 and $149 plans show how often diners view your listing and click to order. These numbers do not track completed orders or sales.

When can I join a paid membership?

Paid enrollment is coming soon. Claim your free listing now and contact us through your restaurant account with any membership questions.
